Scrapers

Jurisdictions

How old each jurisdiction's parish details and service times are, and what it takes to re-read them. A re-run refreshes what nobody has checked and cannot overwrite a pin somebody has confirmed in person.

Archdiocese colours

Map dots and labels always draw these. A parish card draws the parish's own colour, which starts as its jurisdiction's — tick repaint below to carry a change through to the cards that have not been given a colour of their own.

People
